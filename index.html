To my lovely Ashi

My dearest Ashi, 💜

I wish I could sit beside you while you read this. I would probably be smiling like an idiot, watching your expressions and secretly hoping that these words make you blush.

I don't think you realise how beautiful you are to me. And I'm not talking only about your appearance. I'm talking about your heart, your kindness, your personality, your laughter and all those little things that make you the girl I admire so much.

If I could, I would let you see yourself through my eyes for just one minute. Maybe then you would understand why I find your smile so precious, why your eyes make me forget what I was about to say, and why your presence can make an ordinary moment feel unforgettable.

Sometimes I imagine you sitting beside me, your head resting on my shoulder, my hand gently holding yours, while Perfect plays somewhere in the background. I would look at you and probably think, "How did I get so lucky to share this moment with her?"

And if you ever feel insecure, please don't compare yourself with another girl. I don't want a copy of anyone. I don't want a more perfect version of you. I want you to see the beauty in the person you already are.

I want to know your dreams, your little fears, your favourite memories and even the random thoughts that cross your mind. I want to hear about your day, even if nothing interesting happened. Because when it comes to you, even the smallest details matter to me.

I want to hold you close after a long day and remind you that you can breathe, relax and let the world wait for a little while. I want to brush the hair away from your face, look into your eyes and tell you how beautiful you are. And if you get shy, well... I might just smile a little more.

I know I am not perfect. I might make mistakes, sometimes struggle to express myself and still have a lot to learn. But I want to keep becoming someone who makes you feel respected, understood and appreciated. I want my actions to be as beautiful as my promises.

And Ashi, I hope we get to experience those simple dreams: a room full of purple lilies, slow dances, long conversations, sleepy evenings, silly jokes and countless memories. I hope we get to grow together while still becoming our own beautiful selves.

If I could choose one little gift for you, it wouldn't be flowers, jewellery or anything expensive. It would be the ability to see yourself the way I see you, even on the days when you don't feel beautiful.

I would choose your smile on a thousand ordinary mornings. I would choose your laughter on a thousand quiet evenings. And I would choose the opportunity to keep discovering new little things about you.

I cannot tell you exactly what every chapter of the future will look like. But I can tell you that I want to fill the chapters we share with honesty, tenderness, laughter and beautiful memories.

So whenever you hear our song, I hope you remember this: somewhere in this world, there is someone who thinks your existence is a beautiful thing.

Keep blooming, my purple lily. Keep smiling, my favourite girl. And please never forget how precious you are.

With all the warmth in my heart,
Your person, who wishes to keep making you blush. 💜
